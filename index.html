    OPTIONS

    CONTROL STYLE · SWIPE

    For touchscreens. SWIPE turns the car to face the way you swipe - up, down, left or right; a swipe the way you are already going, or straight back, does nothing, since a turn is always a quarter turn. TAP turns left or right with a tap on that half of the screen. AUTO TURN: tap anywhere and it turns whichever way the track goes next - you still choose when. The car drives exactly the same every way, and keys and the mouse are not affected.

    TUTORIAL

    A lap that teaches steering, sliding and finishing, in whatever control style you use, then one real lap at full speed. Nothing in it counts.

    MUSIC · 60

    The menus have a tune, and so does every theme - its three tracks share it. 0 turns the music off.

    SOUND EFFECTS · 80

    Menus, the lights, tyres, walls and rewards. 0 turns them off. M silences everything, music too, until you press it again.

    HIGH CONTRAST · OFF

    Black road, white scenery, and a hard edge round every car. It replaces the palette of every track rather than being a track of its own, and it separates by brightness rather than by colour, so it works whatever your colour vision is. The gates go striped rather than tinted for the same reason. The weather, the painted landscape behind the menus and everything else that is only decoration come off while it is on.

    YOUR CAR · ON

    A halo under your own car, in your own colour. Six cars on a dark track is a lot to pick yourself out of at speed. Your car only, and it changes nothing about the racing.

    DEVELOPER

    SLIDE · 0.80 CELLS

    0 turns slide off entirely. Staircase clips its chicanes above 0.47; much past 3 the arc is wider than the road on any track. [ and ] step it by 0.05, mid-race included.

    OVERSTEER · 0°

    How far the body is drawn leading the way the car is actually travelling while it slides. A look and nothing else: 0 lines the body up with its own direction of travel, 90 throws it fully sideways, and the car goes to exactly the same places at exactly the same speed either way.

    CARS · 6

    You and up to fifteen others. A track grids as many as its road holds, and says so when that is fewer.

    AI LEVEL · 5 NORMAL

    How well the opponents drive, not how fast: 1 makes mistakes constantly, 5 is the normal field, 10 barely puts a wheel wrong. No level ever gives them a higher top speed than yours. Ignored in a time trial.

    GAME SPEED · SWEAT

    RACE LENGTH · 5 LAPS

    ROAD · BLACK

    For trying colour schemes. It changes nothing about the racing.

    LEGACY TRACKS

    The seven circuits built before the themes. They are being replaced, not removed - these are the originals, still raceable.

    YOUR DATA

    Race medals and time-trial lap records. Both go, and resetting them cannot be undone. Your settings, this one included, are left alone.
