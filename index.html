    OPTIONS

    CONTROL STYLE · SWIPE

    For touchscreens. SWIPE turns the car to face the way you swipe - up, down, left or right; a swipe the way you are already going, or straight back, does nothing, since a turn is always a quarter turn. TAP turns left or right with a tap on that half of the screen. AUTO TURN: tap anywhere and it turns whichever way the track goes next - you still choose when. PRO CONTROLS is Auto Turn with a slide: HOLD and the car swings round to face the corner while it keeps sliding at full speed; LET GO and it goes that way. The longer the slide, the bigger the boost when you let go - four levels, each with a flame of its own, and the sparks show which you have reached. Slide too long and you spin out. SUPER SPRINT steers like any racing game: HOLD left or right - a key, or a finger on that side of the screen - and the car turns steadily that way until you let go. The keys and the mouse hold too, under PRO and SUPER SPRINT; otherwise they are not affected.

    SWIPE LENGTH · 24px

    How far your finger has to move before a swipe turns the car. Shorter is more responsive - the turn fires sooner - but a tap that wanders a little may start to count as a swipe. Longer is surer, and later.

    CHAINING · ON

    Keep your finger down and swipe again for the next turn - left, then up, then left - without lifting. Off, every turn is a new swipe.

    TURN SPEED · 240°/s

    Slower is steadier and wants every corner started earlier; faster turns tighter and twitchier. The faster game speeds turn faster in step, so a corner is the same size at every speed. Super Sprint cuts corners a square turn goes round, so its lap records are kept apart and are not sent to the leaderboard.

    BOOST · +20%

    BOOST TIME · 0.2s

    EARLY TURN HELP · ON

    Let go a little before a corner and the car keeps sliding until the corner opens, then turns - instead of turning into its inside wall. Either way, a quick tap turns as close to the moment you pressed as the car can manage.

    TUTORIAL

    A lap that teaches steering, sliding and finishing, in whatever control style you use, then one real lap at full speed. Nothing in it counts.

    MUSIC · 60

    The menus have a tune, and so does every theme - its three tracks share it. 0 turns the music off.

    SOUND EFFECTS · 80

    Menus, the lights, tyres, walls and rewards. 0 turns them off. M silences everything, music too, until you press it again.

    HIGH CONTRAST · OFF

    Black road, white scenery, and a hard edge round every car. It replaces the palette of every track rather than being a track of its own, and it separates by brightness rather than by colour, so it works whatever your colour vision is. The gates go striped rather than tinted for the same reason. The weather, the painted landscape behind the menus and everything else that is only decoration come off while it is on.

    YOUR CAR · ON

    A halo under your own car, in your own colour. Six cars on a dark track is a lot to pick yourself out of at speed. Your car only, and it changes nothing about the racing.

    DEVELOPER

    SLIDE · 0.80 CELLS

    0 turns slide off entirely. Staircase clips its chicanes above 0.47; much past 3 the arc is wider than the road on any track. [ and ] step it by 0.05, mid-race included.

    OVERSTEER · 0°

    How far the body is drawn leading the way the car is actually travelling while it slides. A look and nothing else: 0 lines the body up with its own direction of travel, 90 throws it fully sideways, and the car goes to exactly the same places at exactly the same speed either way.

    CARS · 6

    You and up to fifteen others. A track grids as many as its road holds, and says so when that is fewer.

    AI LEVEL · 5 NORMAL

    How well the opponents drive, not how fast: 1 makes mistakes constantly, 5 is the normal field, 10 barely puts a wheel wrong. No level ever gives them a higher top speed than yours. Ignored in a time trial.
